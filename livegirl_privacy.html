Our privacy commitment

Live Girl collects only information reasonably needed to provide, secure, support, and improve 1v1 video chat, messaging, profiles, social discovery, purchases, moderation, and related features. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

01

Scope & our role

This Policy applies to the Live Girl app, website, and related support channels. Live Girl’s service operator is the controller of personal information processed for these services. It does not cover external services you independently choose to visit.

We design collection around feature necessity. If information is not reasonably needed to run, secure, support, improve, or legally maintain Live Girl, we do not intentionally collect it.

Only share information you are comfortable sharing with other users. Avoid displaying identity documents, payment information, precise home addresses, or other sensitive information during chats or in public profile content.

03

Why we use information

  • Provide accounts, profiles, social discovery, 1v1 video calls, messaging, short videos, likes, saves, follows, rankings, and notifications.
  • Connect users for requested communications and maintain call quality, reliability, and anti-abuse systems.
  • Process purchases, subscriptions, virtual currency, entitlements, and restore transactions.
  • Keep the Service safe, detect fraud or abuse, investigate reports, enforce rules, block prohibited behavior, and protect users and rights.
  • Debug crashes, measure feature reliability, prevent spam, and improve user experience using minimized or aggregated signals where feasible.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, processing may rely on performance of our contract, your consent, legitimate interests in operating a safe and reliable service, or compliance with law. You may withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

04

Video calls, messages & communications

Live Girl processes the technical data needed to establish and maintain real-time communications, such as participant identifiers, connection status, call timestamps, duration, network quality, and abuse-prevention signals.

Audio and video are processed as needed to deliver the communication feature you choose. If a feature stores or records call content, Live Girl will provide appropriate notice and request any permission required by applicable law.

Messages or content you send to another user are visible to that recipient. Content may also be reviewed when it is reported, flagged by safety systems, or otherwise reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce rules, comply with law, or protect users.

05

How information may be disclosed

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose limited information when you direct us to do so, when it is visible to other users by the nature of a feature, when required by valid legal process, or when service providers process data on our behalf to provide hosting, communications, analytics, security, customer support, payment, or similar operational functions.

Service providers are permitted to process information only for authorized purposes and are subject to contractual confidentiality, security, and data-protection obligations. If ownership of Live Girl changes, information may transfer as part of that transaction subject to applicable law and this Policy.

Public means visible. A public profile, photo, video, post, like count, ranking, or other public activity may be viewed, copied, or shared by other users. Use available privacy controls and post thoughtfully.
06

How long information stays

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Account and profile data: while your account is active, then deleted or de-identified after closure, subject to limited legal and safety needs.
  • Messages and user content: until deleted by you, removed by us, or no longer needed for the Service, subject to recipient copies, backups, moderation evidence, and legal obligations.
  • Call metadata: for the period reasonably needed for reliability, fraud prevention, safety investigations, dispute handling, and legal compliance.
  • Purchase, fraud, security, and support records: for applicable tax, chargeback, safety, limitation, and legal periods.
  • Encrypted backups: removed according to the normal backup rotation.

Exact periods may vary by feature and law. You may contact us to ask about the retention period applicable to a specific category of information.

07

Your controls & privacy rights

Use available in-app settings to edit your profile, manage notifications, block users, remove content, manage visibility, or request account deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also ask to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing and to withdraw consent.

Email support@livegirl.com from your account email. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request and will respond within the period required by law. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted. You may appeal a refusal or complain to your local privacy authority where applicable.

We do not use sensitive personal information for unrelated profiling, and we do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely through automated processing.

08

Security, transfers & young users

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as encryption in transit, access controls, logging, minimization, secure development practices, backups, and incident procedures. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected issues promptly. If a breach creates a legally reportable risk, we will notify affected users and authorities as required.

Information may be processed in locations where Live Girl or its service providers operate infrastructure. For cross-border transfers, we use safeguards required by applicable law.

Live Girl is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted information should contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

09

Updates to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect feature, security, operational, or legal changes. We will change the effective date and provide prominent notice when changes materially affect your rights. We will request fresh consent where required by law.
