02 / 11

Information you provide

Depending on your choices, this may include account identifiers, profile name and image, flower interests, saved items, photos, prompts, AI conversations, messages, support emails, reports, and preferences.

Do not submit highly sensitive personal information. If a photo or message contains information about another person, make sure you have permission.

03 / 11

Information created by use

We may process device and app details, language, time zone, app version, approximate network information, crash diagnostics, feature events, fraud signals, and timestamps. We use only what is needed for reliability, security, abuse prevention, and basic product measurement.

Bloomie does not seek precise location, contacts, health data, advertising identifiers, or unrelated browsing history unless a future feature clearly requests permission and this policy is updated first.

04 / 11

How AI data is handled

Bloomie’s AI models are independently developed, trained, deployed, and hosted by us. We do not route prompts, images, or outputs to third-party AI model providers.

Prompts and associated content are processed to return responses, apply safety controls, prevent abuse, and maintain the feature. If we use eligible interactions to improve our models, we minimize identifiers and provide controls or notice required by law. Private messages are not used for unrelated advertising.

05 / 11

Why we process data

We use information to create and maintain accounts; deliver discovery, saving, chat, and AI functions; process coin entitlements; personalize flower suggestions; secure the service; troubleshoot; answer requests; enforce rules; and meet legal obligations.

Where required, our legal bases include performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests in a safe and functional service, and compliance with law. You may withdraw consent for future processing where consent applies.

06 / 11

No sale, trade, rent, or sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. We do not provide personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or third-party AI model services.

Limited access by infrastructure, payment, app-marketplace, security, or support vendors may occur only as processors acting under our instructions to operate Bloomie. They are contractually restricted, receive the minimum necessary data, and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose data if legally compelled or necessary to protect life, safety, or rights.

07 / 11

Retention & deletion

We retain account and feature data only as long as needed for the purposes described, then delete or de-identify it. Timeframes vary: active account content lasts while your account is open; routine diagnostics are generally short-lived; purchase and legal records may remain as required by tax, fraud, or other law.

Deletion requests are verified and completed within the period required by applicable law. Backups age out on a controlled schedule. We may retain narrowly scoped records when required for disputes, security, or legal compliance.

08 / 11

Security

We use layered safeguards such as encryption in transit, appropriate encryption at rest, least-privilege access, authentication controls, logging, testing, backups, and incident response. No system can guarantee absolute security.

If a breach creates a legally reportable risk, we will investigate, mitigate, and notify affected people and authorities as required.

09 / 11

Your choices & rights

You can edit profile information, manage notifications and device permissions, remove saved content, delete conversations where available, or request account deletion. Depending on location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent.

Email support@bloomie.work to exercise a right. We may verify identity and will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request. Authorized-agent requests must include valid authority.

10 / 11

Children

Bloomie is not directed to children below the minimum age permitted for independent digital consent in their country. We do not knowingly collect their personal information without valid guardian authorization. Contact us if you believe a child submitted information improperly.

11 / 11

International use & updates

Data may be processed where Bloomie operates its controlled systems and vendors. We use legally required transfer safeguards. Local privacy rights remain available.

We will revise this policy when practices or law materially change, update the effective date, and provide appropriate notice. Prior versions may be requested from support.
